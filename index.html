About Couch Kick-Off

Couch Kick-Off is an independent guide to men's football on UK TV. It isn't connected to any broadcaster, league or club; channel names are shown only so you know where each match is on.

The listings are checked every morning and audited every week, but TV picks and kick-off times can change at short notice, so check with the broadcaster on the day.

No accounts and no adverts. Your teams and filters are saved only in your own browser. We use privacy-friendly Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how people use Couch Kick-Off. It doesn't use cookies or identify individual visitors.

Spotted a mistake? Tap Report on the match, or email hello@couchkickoff.co.uk.

Install the app

Add Couch Kick-Off to your home screen and it opens full screen, like any other app. There's nothing to download or update.

  • Android: open it in Chrome, tap ⋮ then Install app (or Add to home screen).
  • iPhone: open it in Safari, tap Share then Add to Home Screen.

Rest of the season

Premier League teams show every fixture. Games kicking off at 3pm on a Saturday are marked "Not on UK TV" because of the blackout. For other leagues, the listed games are the ones with confirmed or expected UK TV coverage, plus games that fall in the blackout.

Every Premier League fixture up to the final day (30 May) is listed. From mid-January most of them say "TV pick TBC" because Sky and TNT haven't chosen them yet. Usually around 6 or 7 of each weekend's 10 games get moved for TV. The rest stay at 3pm on Saturday and aren't shown live in the UK. There are two exceptions: TNT's deal covers all 20 games in the 10 Feb and 3 Mar midweek rounds, and Sky shows all 10 games on the final day.

Cup and European rounds are listed by date until the ties are drawn.

A solid channel badge means the broadcaster has confirmed that match. An outlined badge marked Expected means the broadcaster holds the rights to the competition but hasn't confirmed that particular game yet. It turns solid once it's confirmed. TV games only shows both confirmed and expected games, and hides games marked "TV pick TBC" or "Not on UK TV".

  • Premier League: every TV pick to 7 Jan. The rest of January is due the week of 30 Nov.
  • EFL (Sky Sports): to 9 Jan. Sky also streams most 3pm Saturday EFL games on Sky Sports+.

  • Champions League and Europa League: league phase to 27/28 Jan, all on TNT Sports. Prime Video takes one Tuesday Champions League game each week, but most of its picks haven't been announced.
  • Conference League: to 17 Dec (TNT Sports).
  • Carabao Cup quarter-finals: week of 14 Dec on Sky Sports. Exact dates and times aren't confirmed yet.
  • LaLiga: every fixture to the end of the season is listed. Kick-off times are set to matchday 14 (27 to 30 Nov), plus two matchday 23 games brought forward to 2 Dec; later games show their weekend until LaLiga sets the times. Premier Sports shows most games, Disney+ one Saturday night game. Saturday 3:15pm games can't be shown live.
  • Serie A: every fixture to the end of the season is listed, with kick-off times to 11 Jan. All on DAZN. BBC ALBA and iPlayer also show about one a week (their picks aren't listed). Saturday 2pm games fall in the blackout.
  • Bundesliga: every fixture to the end of the season is listed, with kick-off times to 13 Dec. Fridays free on BBC iPlayer, Saturday 5:30pm "top match" on Sky Sports, all Sunday games on Prime Video pay-per-view. Saturday 2:30pm games have no UK broadcaster.
  • Not yet announced: FA Cup second round (5 to 7 Dec) and third round (January) picks, and the February to May picks for every competition. Internationals resume in late March 2027.
